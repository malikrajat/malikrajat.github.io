Privacy policy

This policy explains what rajatmalik.dev collects, why, who processes it, and the choices available to you — including how to control advertising and analytics cookies.

Effective 17 November 2026 Last updated 17 November 2026 India

On this page

In plain language: this is a personal portfolio and article site. I collect the information you choose to send me, privacy-friendly visit counts, and — with your consent where required — advertising and analytics data through Google. I do not sell your personal information.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for rajatmalik.dev is Rajat Malik, an individual publisher based in Gurgaon, Haryana, India. For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact contact@rajatmalik.dev.

This policy applies to rajatmalik.dev and its subpages. It does not apply to third-party sites that this site links to; those sites have their own policies.

2. What is collected and why

Information you provide

  • Contact form and email. If you use the contact form or email me, I receive your name, email address, subject, the type of enquiry you select and your message. The form is processed by Formspree and delivered to me by email. I use this information only to read and respond to your enquiry, and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • Resume download. The PDF is served as a static file. No personal information is required to download it.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit analytics (GoatCounter). I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and understand which pages are useful. It records the page path, the referring site, approximate country, browser and screen information, and a rotating identifier used to estimate unique visits. It is designed not to create a persistent profile of individuals and does not set advertising cookies. See the GoatCounter privacy notice.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, for security, abuse prevention and service operation. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Tag management and measurement. The site loads Google Tag Manager (container GTM-K533LRTH), which may load Google Analytics and other measurement tags configured in that container. When active and permitted, these tools may collect identifiers, device and browser details, approximate location and interaction data. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Local browser storage. A small preference (your cookie choice) and, where GoatCounter totals are unavailable, a local visit counter are stored in your browser. These stay on your device and are not sent to me as personal data.

Embedded and linked services

Some pages load content or resources from third parties. When those resources load, the provider may receive your IP address and request metadata:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces used across the site.
  • Google Maps — an embedded map in the contact section of the home page.
  • Simple Icons CDN — brand icons in the writing section.
  • Social and publishing platforms — LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, DEV, Substack, X, Threads, Bluesky, Telegram and npm, when you follow a link to them.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. The table below describes the categories that may be present; the exact cookies set by Google depend on your region, consent and the ads that are served.

Category Purpose Examples Control
Strictly necessary / preference Remembers your cookie choice and keeps the site working. rm.consent.v1 (local storage) Cannot be disabled without losing your preference; clearing site data removes it.
Analytics Counts visits and helps me understand which content is useful. GoatCounter (no advertising cookies); Google Analytics cookies such as _ga when enabled and permitted. Reject non-essential storage, or use the opt-outs below.
Advertising Serves and measures ads through Google AdSense, limits repetition and, with consent, personalises ads. Cookies set by Google and its partners, which may include IDE, NID, __gads, __gpi, __eoi or similar names. Consent choice, Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info, browser controls.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may affect parts of this site and other sites.

  • Legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the site, responding to enquiries, and understanding aggregate readership, balanced against your rights.
  • Pre-contractual steps or contract where you contact me about a role, engagement or other request and I need to respond or take agreed steps.
  • Legal obligations where I must retain or disclose information to comply with the law.

7. How long data is kept

  • Contact enquiries: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted unless a continuing relationship or legal obligation requires longer.
  • Aggregate analytics: retained by the analytics provider according to its settings and used only in aggregate.
  • Server logs: retained by the hosting provider for a short period for security and abuse prevention.
  • Consent preference: kept in your browser until you clear site data or change it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you can complain to your national supervisory authority. In India, you may raise a grievance with the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant mechanism applies.

United States (California and other states)

If you are in California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may request access, deletion, correction and information about processing, and you may opt out of sale or sharing. I do not sell personal information. You can exercise the opt-out with this link: Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. To exercise any right, email contact@rajatmalik.dev; I may need to verify your identity before acting. You will not be discriminated against for making a request.

Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) may apply. I process the limited data described in this policy for the purposes and legal bases set out above, including consent for advertising and analytics storage where required. You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and revocation of consent, and you may complain to the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). Google’s controls for Brazilian traffic include the choice between personalised and non-personalised ads; where required, the site’s consent message records that choice. Requests can be sent to contact@rajatmalik.dev.

9. Children and age-restricted treatment

This site is a professional portfolio aimed at adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (the age used by the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) or under 16 where a higher age applies, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The content is general-audience professional material, not child-directed content, so no child-directed ad treatment is requested. I do not use interest-based advertising or remarketing to target children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and is statically hosted, which reduces the attack surface. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect the limited data described here. Please do not send sensitive personal, financial or health information through the contact form.
