Editorial standards

These articles are written by Rajat Malik and published on this site. They are based on first-hand engineering work, documentation and testing, not on scraped or auto-generated summaries. Where an article states a measurement or an outcome, it comes from a real project; where it states an opinion, it is labelled as one.

  • Original: every article is written for this site and is not republished from another publication.
  • Reviewed: code samples and recommendations are checked against current framework documentation before publication.
  • Corrections: if something is wrong or out of date, email contact@rajatmalik.dev and the article will be corrected with a note.
  • Independent: no article is sponsored, and advertising is served separately from the editorial content. See the advertising disclosure.
