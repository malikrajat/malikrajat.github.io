What I do

I am a frontend architect and lead engineer. My work usually lands in one of four places:

  • Modernising legacy platforms without stalling the roadmap — moving older Angular and React applications to current versions, safer module boundaries and a delivery process the team can trust.
  • Architecture for independent delivery — design systems, micro-frontend boundaries and shared contracts that let multiple teams ship without coordinating every release.
  • Performance and accessibility where they affect revenue and retention: Core Web Vitals, rendering strategy, bundle discipline and WCAG 2.2 at component level.
  • Engineering quality — testing strategy, code review, release checks and mentoring, so the improvement survives after the initiative ends.

How I work

  • Make the problem measurable. Agree the user-visible outcome and the number that proves it before choosing a solution.
  • Prefer removing work to micro-optimising it. Less JavaScript, fewer third parties and simpler state usually beat clever code.
  • Design for the team that maintains it. An architecture that only its author understands is a liability, not a standard.
  • Leave the guard rails behind. Tests, budgets and review checklists matter more than a one-off clean-up.
  • Be direct about trade-offs. I would rather give a clear recommendation and its cost than a list of options with no opinion.
